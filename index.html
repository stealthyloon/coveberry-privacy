COVEBERRY

Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026 · Publisher: YettyTech, LLC

Who this app is for

Coveberry is a calm pet-monster learning app aimed primarily at children ages 4–7, with a parent-controlled Parent Area. We do not show ads to children.

What we store on the device

Pet name, care stats, Learn progress, parent settings, and (if you add it) the Home Screen widget share local data via an App Group on the same device. This information stays on the device unless you back up the device with Apple services you control.

What we do not collect from children

We do not ask children for email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or social account logins. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Subscriptions

Optional Learn unlocks are sold as a parent-gated Apple subscription ($2.99/month). Purchases are processed by Apple. Kids only see “Ask a grown-up” and never a payment sheet. Apple may process purchase records under Apple’s privacy policy. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Analytics

v1 does not include third-party analytics SDKs. If we add parent-only, Kids-Category-compliant analytics later, this policy will be updated before that change ships.

Contact

Privacy questions for Coveberry: email brandon@yetty.tech with subject “Coveberry privacy,” or use the contact form at yetty.tech.
